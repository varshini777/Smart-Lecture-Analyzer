# Research Data Collection Protocol

**Lecture Engagement Pattern Analyzer**  
*(Classroom Behavioral Observation and Analysis System)*

---

## 1. Ethics, Consent, & Participant Privacy

1. **Informed Consent**: Prior to beginning any data collection session intended for research or machine learning model development, the participant must provide voluntary, informed consent.
2. **Anonymous Identification**:
   - Participants must be assigned an arbitrary, non-identifying research code (e.g., `P001`, `P002`, `SUBJ_05`).
   - Names, email addresses, student IDs, or any identifiable information are strictly prohibited as participant codes.
3. **Decoupling of Tracking IDs**:
   - The visual tracking ID (`S001`, `S002`) generated by the computer-vision tracker is transient and session-local.
   - It is not tied to biometric descriptors and does not persist across separate sessions.
4. **Local Data Retention & Right to Erasure**:
   - All session observations and optional recordings are stored locally under `data/database/` and `data/recordings/`.
   - Participants have the right to request deletion of their sessions at any time.

---

## 2. Hardware Setup & Environmental Configuration

- **Camera Placement**: Position the webcam at approximately eye level (or slightly above, angled 5°–10° downward) facing the participant desk area.
- **Lighting**: Ensure even, diffuse lighting across the subject's face. Avoid direct backlighting from windows or bright overhead spotlights.
- **Distance**: The participant should sit between 0.6 m and 2.5 m from the camera lens, ensuring the face occupies at least 5% to 20% of the total frame area.
- **Resolution**: Standard resolution is configured at 1280x720 (720p). Ensure camera settings are validated via `python scripts/check_environment.py --probe-camera`.

---

## 3. Observation Protocols

### 3.1 Controlled Observation Protocol (Calibration & Verification)
In controlled sessions, a consenting participant executes specific behavioral actions under structured time intervals to verify perceptual sensitivity:
1. **00:00 – 01:00**: Upright forward-facing attention (`ENGAGED_BEHAVIOR`).
2. **01:00 – 02:00**: Turn head deliberately to the left (30°–45° yaw) for 20s, then right for 20s (`LOOKING_AWAY`).
3. **02:00 – 03:00**: Lower head downward while writing or typing on desk (`WRITING_READING`).
4. **03:00 – 04:00**: Rest head on hand or lower head without desk interaction (`HEAD_DOWN`).
5. **04:00 – 05:00**: Hold and interact with a smartphone in lap or at desk level (`PHONE_USE`).
6. **05:00 – 05:30**: Safe eye closure demonstration while upright (`DROWSY_SLEEP_LIKE`).

### 3.2 Naturalistic Observation Protocol (Unscripted Classroom Sessions)
- Participants engage in normal lecture listening, note-taking, and classroom activities.
- The operator does **not** prescribe or enforce specific behavioral sequences.
- Sessions should ideally run between 10 minutes and 45 minutes to capture authentic behavioral variations.

---

## 4. Post-Session Quality Audit Procedure

Immediately following session completion:
1. Open the **Sessions and Reports** or **Dataset & Quality Audit** tab.
2. Select the completed session and click **Run Quality Audit**.
3. Verify that the session receives a **`PASS`** or **`PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`** verdict:
   - Check that effective FPS is >= 15.
   - Check that timestamp jitter standard deviation is < 150 ms.
   - Ensure unobservable frames do not exceed 40%.
4. If the verdict is `FAIL` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, inspect the reported reasons (e.g., lighting failure, camera disconnect) and document any anomalies.
